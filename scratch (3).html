What is Brawl Stars?

Brawl Stars is a fast-paced multiplayer mobile game developed by Supercell, released globally in December 2018. Players choose from a roster of unique characters called Brawlers, each with distinct attacks and abilities. Matches are quick — typically 2–3 minutes — making it perfect for on-the-go gaming.

With over 80+ Brawlers, 15+ game modes, and seasonal events, there's always something new to master. Whether you prefer solo play, duo matches, or 3v3 team fights, Brawl Stars has a mode for you.

Why Players Love Brawl Stars

  1. Fast and accessible gameplay
    • Matches last only 2–3 minutes
    • Easy to learn, hard to master
    • Free to play on iOS and Android
  2. Diverse Brawler roster
    • Common, Rare, Super Rare, Epic, Mythic, and Legendary rarities
    • Each Brawler has unique stats, a Super, and Gadgets
    • New Brawlers added every season
  3. Competitive depth
    • Ranked mode with progressive tiers
    • World Championship esports circuit
    • Club League for team competition

Meet the Brawlers

Each Brawler belongs to a class that defines their role in battle.

🐂

Tank

High health, close-range damage. Charges into enemies and dominates tight spaces.

Example: Bull
🔫

Sharpshooter

Long range, high damage. Stays back and picks off enemies from a distance.

Example: Colt
⚡

Assassin

Fast, high burst damage. Dashes in, eliminates a target, and escapes quickly.

Example: Leon
💚

Support

Heals allies and controls the battlefield. Essential for coordinated team play.

Example: Poco
🌀

Controller

Area control specialists. Spreads attacks across zones to deny enemy movement.

Example: Spike
💣

Thrower

Lobs projectiles over walls. Hard to counter if hidden, weak up close.

Example: Dynamike

Game Modes

💎

Gem Grab

3v3. Collect 10 gems from the center mine and hold them for a countdown to win.

💀

Brawl Ball

3v3 soccer-style. Score 2 goals before the enemy team to win the match.

🏆

Showdown

Solo or Duo battle royale. Be the last Brawler standing on a shrinking map.

🔐

Heist

3v3. Attack the enemy safe while defending your own. Pure offensive chaos.

🌟

Hot Zone

3v3. Capture and hold zones on the map longer than the enemy team.

🎯

Bounty

3v3. Earn stars by eliminating enemies. The team with the most stars wins.

Pro Tips for New Players

01

Push bushes carefully

Enemies often hide in bushes waiting to ambush. Tap ahead of you to check before walking in blind.

02

Match your Brawler to the mode

Tanks excel in Brawl Ball. Sharpshooters shine in Bounty. Always check the recommended Brawlers before queuing.

03

Save your Super strategically

Don't waste your Super immediately. Hold it for key moments like breaking open a safe or escaping danger.

04

Play with friends in a Club

Club League rewards are incredible. Playing with coordinated teammates doubles your win rate and fun.
